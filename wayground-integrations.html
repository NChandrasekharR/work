Overview

Wayground's LMS and platform integrations are the plumbing every enterprise deal runs through. Districts adopt the product through Canvas, Schoology, and MS Teams, and admins judge the product by how those connectors behave. The legacy integrations held a stability of 86%, built on aging protocols, and enterprise customers were asking for standards-based connectors we couldn't offer.

I owned the migration experience: admin setup flows, error and fallback states, and the change management that moved 100% of customers to the new connectors in a single cutover. The stability number is a team outcome I contributed to. The zero-churn cutover is the design and communications outcome I can claim.

By April 2025, integration stability stood at 99%, every customer had cut over with zero churn, $1.3M in paid accounts ran the MS Teams integration, and the integrations work opened the UK, Australia, and Canada for sales.
